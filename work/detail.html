01 / Overview

A comprehensive restructuring of digital asset management for a high-end editorial house. The project sought to bridge the gap between temporary digital publication and the permanence of physical print through a custom-built archival engine.

02 / Role

Lead Product Designer & Design Systems Architect. Responsible for the end-to-end user experience, from high-level conceptualization to the final implementation of the typographic system and interface components.

03 / Challenge

The existing ecosystem was fragmented. Content lived in silos, and the "Editorial Voice" was lost in a sea of generic CMS templates. We needed to create a system that felt as authoritative as a monograph but remained flexible enough for daily digital updates.

  • / 01 Fragmented archival workflows across multiple departments.
  • / 02 Erosion of brand identity in high-density information environments.
  • / 03 Technical limitations of standard grid-based layouts.

04 / Solution

We introduced a "Paper-First" digital philosophy. By utilizing Space Grotesk in a mono-spaced rhythm and removing traditional UI chrome, we forced the user to focus entirely on the content. The navigation was moved to contextual touchpoints, allowing the editorial work to breathe in expansive whitespace.

05 / Outcome

A 40% increase in reader retention time and a complete transformation of the brand's digital perception. The system has since been adopted as the primary framework for all future archival projects.
